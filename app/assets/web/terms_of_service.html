Eligibility

Renly is intended for real estate negotiators registered with the Board of Valuers, Appraisers, Estate Agents and Property Managers Malaysia (REN). Account approval requires REN verification.

Co-broking conduct

Listings and requirements must be genuine. Commission splits agreed in-app are between the two negotiators; Renly is not a party to the transaction and does not guarantee any deal's completion.

Account suspension

Accounts found posting fraudulent listings, misrepresenting REN status, or abusing other negotiators may be suspended without notice.

Subscription & billing

Professional-tier subscriptions are billed via Stripe on a recurring basis and may be cancelled at any time through the Subscription screen's billing portal.

See also our Privacy Policy, the Commission Calculator, or email support@renly.app.
